GOG Xray-only migration v2.09-xray-only

This public manual update migrates ordinary GOG sing-box v2.07-sb1137 routers
to the Xray-only runtime. It preserves each router's saved subscription and
settings, stops the old sing-box runtime, installs bundled Xray and lite
Re:filter assets, rebuilds the runtime from the saved payload, validates Xray,
and leaves future update checks on the Xray-only manifest line.

During this migration the old updater may appear to time out because the core
replacement can take longer than the old HTTP request lifetime on weak routers.
Wait a minute or two, refresh the page, and confirm the app reports
v2.09-xray-only / no_update.
