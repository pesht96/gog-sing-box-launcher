GOG Sing-Box Launcher v2.53-sb1137

Automatic quality selection now also runs while the current node is reachable.
The three-minute watchdog compares the active node and up to two rotating
selected alternatives with short, bounded YouTube transfer samples.

A switch requires two comparisons with at least 30 percent and 32768 B/s
improvement, and a candidate throughput of at least 65536 B/s. Close scores
retain the current node. Manual Ping remains available, has a three-minute
grace period, and automatic decisions do not overwrite its saved preference.
Operator actions take priority; a failed post-switch check restores the old
member unless the operator intervened. Existing connections are not forcibly
interrupted and sing-box is not restarted for quality selection.

Active-node decisions and status now prefer the actual Clash selector, not
the last outbound observation in the log. Opening the UI remains observational.

Preserves each router's subscription, selected pool, manual domains, route and
DNS policy. The package contains application code only, no operator VPN nodes
or credentials. Signed control_plane update; manual and opted-in auto-install.

Verified locally and on the live router, including real automatic handover,
checkbox Apply/reload round-trip and sequential health/UI/12-pass stress.
This is not a guarantee of external-node speed, country eligibility or
uninterrupted multi-day video playback. Existing DNS saturation warnings remain
a separate investigation, not claimed fixed by this release.
