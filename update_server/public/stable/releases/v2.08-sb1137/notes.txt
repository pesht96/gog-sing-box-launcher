GOG Sing-Box Launcher v2.08-sb1137

This ordinary sing-box control-plane update adds a narrow crypto-services
route preset to the existing foreign-services option.

Trust Wallet, Bybit, and MEXC service domains are now routed through
AUTO-BALANCE when foreign-services routing is enabled. The same route works for
Android and iPhone clients that use the router as their gateway.

The update does not bundle VPN servers, does not replace the saved
subscription URL, does not change selected nodes, and does not alter manual
domains or routing settings already stored on the router.
